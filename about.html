About PawSOS

Bridging the Gap in Animal Emergency Care

PawSOS provides a public way to record animal distress reports and follow status changes. It is not an emergency dispatch service.

How PawSOS Operates

PawSOS records reports that can be reviewed by authorized responders. There is no automatic dispatch notification, verified clinic directory, or guaranteed response time in this deployment.

When you allow location access, the report records the location and accuracy provided by your browser. Public maps use approximate pins; authorized responders can view the operational location.

Field Guidelines

While waiting for help

General safety guidance only. PawSOS does not diagnose injuries or replace veterinary advice.

🚗

Keep yourself safe

  • Stay out of traffic and other immediate hazards.
  • Keep a safe distance; a frightened or injured animal may react defensively.
  • Do not attempt to restrain or move the animal unless a qualified professional advises you and it is safe.
☀️

Share useful details

  • Note a nearby landmark and the direction the animal is moving, if safe.
  • Keep children and pets at a distance.
  • Do not give food, water, or medication unless a veterinary professional advises it.
🕊️

For trapped or wild animals

  • Do not enter drains, climb, cut entanglements, or attempt a rescue that could injure you or the animal.
  • Keep the area calm and contact a local qualified rescue or veterinary service directly.
  • Let responders know about hazards, access restrictions, or movement you observe.
