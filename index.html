What to Do While Waiting for Help

Simple guidelines to keep both you and the animal safe at the scene.

1. Stay at a Safe Distance

Injured animals are frightened and may bite out of self-defense. Do not corner them or make sudden movements. Keep children and other pets away.

2. Do Not Force Food or Water

Never pour liquids into the mouth of an animal that is in shock, lying down, or unconscious. Liquid can enter the windpipe and cause choking.

3. Keep the Animal in Sight

If possible, remain nearby until the responder arrives so you can point out the exact spot. Animals often try to crawl into hiding spots.
